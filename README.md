# master-of-agents (moa)

A keyboard-first home for coding agents. moa runs your Claude (and other) agent sessions
in tmux, keeps every conversation resumable by ID, and has a central agent, **moa**, that
watches what your sessions do. It builds a picture of your projects and tasks, tells you
what needs attention, and can open, resume and tidy sessions for you.

**Status: design phase.** `spike/` holds throwaway prototypes we use to find out what to
build. `spike/LEARNINGS.md` records what they taught us. The real implementation will
follow an approved design in `docs/`.
